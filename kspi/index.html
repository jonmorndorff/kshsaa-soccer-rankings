Scoring standards

Each game earns points from adjusted opponent strength (75% opponent winning percentage + 25% the average winning percentage of that opponent's opponents) and the result. Away wins and losses earn one more point than home games; neutral sites use the midpoint. Ties earn the midpoint of win and loss points. Classification adjustment: +1 per higher class faced, or −1 per lower class, capped at 2 points. These classification adjustments are experimental, not part of the KSPI scoring matrix.

Index = average game points, not cumulative points. SOS uses the same 75% / 25% opponent-strength formula, averaged over each team’s schedule. Opponents with no matched schedule use their own winning percentage for the second component. KSPI adjusts close results against significantly stronger or weaker opponents. When your win percentage exceeds your opponent by at least 30 percentage points, a 1-goal win loses 3 points, a 2-goal win loses 2, and a 3-goal win loses 1; a 4+ goal win receives full base points. At a 15–29 point advantage, a 1-goal win loses 2 and a 2-goal win loses 1. A close loss against a substantially stronger opponent can earn up to 2 bonus points. Margins are capped at 5 goals, and no additional credit is given for running up the score. Unverified external opponents are excluded from index calculations rather than assigned invented records.
