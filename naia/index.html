Scoring standards

Each game earns points from the opponent's winning percentage and the result. Away wins and losses earn one more point than home games; neutral sites use the midpoint. Ties earn the midpoint of win and loss points. Classification adjustment: +1 per higher class faced, or −1 per lower class, capped at 2 points. These classification adjustments are experimental, not part of the KSPI scoring matrix.
