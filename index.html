Opponent-adjusted performance

Scores are calculated iteratively using opponents and opponents' opponents. Goal margins are capped at five to reduce incentives to run up scores. Opponent-adjusted performance compares the actual capped goal margin against the expected margin from team strength. Narrow wins over weaker opponents earn full win credit but less performance credit. Close losses to stronger opponents can receive higher performance credit than blowout losses. A loss never counts as a win.

Ratings are model estimates on a 0–100 scale, not probabilities of winning. Small sample sizes can cause volatile rankings. All classes are ranked together before filtering by classification.

Official source policy
